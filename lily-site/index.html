A FEW HELPFUL ANSWERS

Before you
head away.

How does the meet-and-greet work?

It’s free and preferably in your home. We’ll get acquainted and review feeding, medications, walks, playtime, and household tasks before confirming a booking.

Can I get updates and photos?

Yes. Photo updates are available upon request. We’ll discuss how often you’d like updates and how you’d like to receive them.

Can Lily help with medications or shy pets?

Lily has experience with oral, injected, topical, and eye-drop medications, and is patient with shy or reclusive pets. We’ll review instructions and any special care needs before agreeing to a booking.

What’s included, and how long does care last?

Care can include meals, fresh water, habitat upkeep, playtime or walks, medications, and agreed household tasks. Visit lengths, walking times, and overnight arrangements are agreed with you before booking.

Lily is most comfortable with cats and dogs. Other animals and unfamiliar routines need a conversation before care is accepted.

How should I prepare for care?

Have feeding and medication instructions, an emergency contact, and your veterinarian’s contact details ready. Tell Lily about escape tendencies so you can discuss a plan at the meet-and-greet.

Arrange keys and access information privately. Please mention indoor cameras and any visitors or contractors expected while Lily is at your home. Keep access codes and full home addresses out of the inquiry form.

Your designated contact will be alerted about injuries, illness, or severe weather.

What happens if plans change?

Cancellations within 48 hours of the scheduled start may carry a one-day care fee. Discuss your booking’s terms with Lily before confirming.

If Lily becomes unavailable or must stop while your pet is in her care, there is no charge for services already rendered.
