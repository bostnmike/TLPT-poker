Boston Bruins · 2022–23 · Games 75–82

The Road to History

Eight games. Eight wins. A finish for the record books. From the Presidents’ Trophy clincher on March 30 to the season finale in Montréal on April 13, this collection preserves every game of Boston’s final push.

These nine artifacts tell a team story: game-used pucks, goal pucks from Marchand’s teammates, and both a Warm-Up Puck and a Game-Used Puck from April 2 in St. Louis. No Brad Marchand goal puck is represented in this chapter. Together, they trace the Bruins’ march to a 65–12–5 record and 135 points—the most wins and points in a single NHL regular season.
