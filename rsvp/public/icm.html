    What’s the math doing?

    ICM values each remaining chip stack against the original prizes, assuming equal playing ability. It uses the whole remaining prize pool; bounties and money already paid out stay separate.

    Results are shown to the cent. Any fractional pennies are allocated so the payouts add up exactly. These are proposed chop amounts to review with the table.
