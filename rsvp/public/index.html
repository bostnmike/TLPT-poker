Add TLPT RSVP

Your next game, one tap away. Keep the name TLPT RSVP when you add it.

This shortcut opens Commish view. After adding it, open the new icon. If you see Unlock Commish, paste your private link from Bitwarden there to sign in.

On iPhone or iPad

  1. Open this app in your web browser. If you scanned a QR code inside another app, open its menu and choose Open in browser.
  2. Tap Share (the square with an upward arrow). You may find it inside the browser’s menu.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen. If it is missing, look under Edit Actions.
  4. Leave Open as Web App on, if shown, then tap Add.
  5. Open the new TLPT RSVP icon on your Home Screen.

On Android

  1. Open this app in your web browser. If you scanned inside another app, choose Open in browser from its menu.
  2. Open the browser’s menu, usually shown as three dots or three lines.
  3. Choose Add to Home screen or Install app. If you see Install and create shortcut, choose it, then tap Install.
  4. Confirm the phone’s prompt, then open the TLPT RSVP icon.

On a computer

  1. Open this app in your web browser.
  2. Look for Install TLPT RSVP or Install app in the address bar or browser menu. On a Mac, you may find Add to Dock in the File menu.
  3. Confirm the browser’s prompt, then open TLPT RSVP from your apps.

If your browser has no install option, try Safari on iPhone or iPad, Chrome on Android, or Chrome or Edge on a computer. Safari on a Mac also offers Add to Dock.

Internet is needed for live lineups and saving RSVPs. Once the app opens, pick your name and tap In, Maybe or Out.
