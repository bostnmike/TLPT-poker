Add TLPT RSVP

Your next game, one tap away. Keep the name TLPT RSVP when you add it.

On iPhone or iPad

  1. Open this app in Safari. If you scanned a QR code inside another app, open its menu and choose Open in Safari.
  2. Tap Share (the square with an upward arrow). In Safari’s compact layout, open the Page Menu first.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen. If it is missing, scroll to Edit Actions and add that action.
  4. Leave Open as Web App on, if shown, then tap Add.
  5. Open the new TLPT RSVP icon on your Home Screen.

On Android

  1. Open this app in Chrome. If you scanned inside another app, choose Open in Chrome from its menu.
  2. Tap Chrome’s three-dot menu beside the address bar.
  3. Choose Install and create shortcut → Install. Some versions call this Install app or Add to Home screen.
  4. Confirm the phone’s prompt, then open the TLPT RSVP icon.

On a computer

  1. In Chrome or Edge, look for Install TLPT RSVP in the address bar or browser menu.
  2. On a Mac in Safari, choose File → Add to Dock.
  3. Confirm the browser’s prompt, then open TLPT RSVP from your apps.

Internet is needed for live lineups and saving RSVPs. Once the app opens, pick your name and tap In, Maybe or Out.
