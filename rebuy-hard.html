’Twas the Night Before Re-Buy Hard

Download the handout ↓ Read the poem
A♠ The Room ↗ K♦ The Felt ↗ Q♣ The Bounties ↗

’Twas the night before Re-Buy Hard, all through the house, Not a nit could be stirring, not even a mouse; Two tables stood ready, their chairs set with care, For sixteen poor suckers who'd soon gather there.

The chips were nestled all snug in their racks, With visions of check raises and super deep stacks. And I with my laptop, the stats, and my pen, Had just settled down, to "League Roast" again.

When down in the Caaahhd Room there rose such a clatter, I sprang from my bed to see what was the matter. Away to the Foyer Room I flew like a flash, Checked Venmo for buy-ins and totaled the cash.

The DeckMate lay waiting, its lights all aglow, With a dozen fresh coolers all ready to go; When what to my wondering eyes should appear, But eight poker donkeys, all trembling with fear.

With a new app, an iPad, and one final wish, I knew in a moment it must be the Commish. More rapid than rockets his donkeys they came, And he whistled and shouted and called them by name:

“Now, Red! and now, Li-Fo! now, Hiro and Chris! On, NASA! on, Cougar! on, Liam and Vish! To the flop! to the turn! To the river with dread! Now shove away! Jam even if you're Card Dead!”

As the short stacks went flying when blinds climbed too high, Kings and Aces got cracked, they kissed chips goodbye; So up to the cashier the donkeys they flew, With a fistful of rebuys and bounty plaques too.

And then, in a moment, I heard from the lot, A clamor for coffee, much more than one pot. Then I turned from the kitchen and spun right around, As Bambinos had landed with one mighty bound.

He was dressed all in red, from his head to his foot, With samosa-stained cuffs and a dusting of soot; A pile of pizzas he'd slung on his back, As Griffin stood ready to purchase the stack.

His eyes—how they twinkled! His grin—how it spread! "Nine seconds to rebuy?" he chuckled at Red. His droll little mouth was drawn up like a bow, "Poor Dave! Every river's a nut-cracking blow."

BostnMike had a list that was clenched in his teeth, "Berserk beats Berserker" was scrawled underneath; He had a broad face and a little round belly, That shook when he laughed, like a bowl full of jelly.

He was clever and sly, like a mischievous elf, He’d invited more card sharks just to punish himself; A wink of his eye and a shake of his head, Said, “The DeckMate hates Providence—and wants him card dead!”

He spoke not a word, but went straight to his work, While collecting the rebuys, he turned with a jerk; As Norm and Ali were flambaying the room, He smiled to himself, then he lowered the boom.

He sprang to his station, with bounties to count, While the donkeys kept paying to swell the amount; But I heard him exclaim, ere he drove out of sight: “Happy Re-Buy Hard to all, and to all a good night!”
