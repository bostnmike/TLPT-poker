TLPT Cashier

Add the poker-chip app to your iPad.

In Safari on your iPad

  1. Stay on this page and tap Share (the square with an upward arrow).
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen. It may be under More or View More.
  3. Leave Open as Web App on, if shown. Keep the name TLPT Cashier and check that the preview shows the yellow chip.
  4. Tap Add. Return to the Home Screen and open the new TLPT Cashier icon.

The app opens in its own window, without browser tabs or an address bar. Sign in with your existing cashier password if asked. There is no need to tap Full screen.

Keep your previous shortcut until the new one works. Adding the app does not change your payments. An internet connection is still required. Open Cashier
