Welcome to the Heater Meter. This answers, “How have you been playing lately?” Not, “How fondly do you remember that one night?”

Players qualify after four matching appearances. The meter uses up to their last six.

Hot and Cold track the direction of finishing depth. Consistent balances form score against swings. Volatile finds the roller coasters. None of those buttons measures how confidently somebody announces a raise.

Deeper finishes and knockouts add heat. Rebuys and early exits cool things down. More recent events carry more weight.

Choose an event format or bounty filter to narrow the comparison. If nobody qualifies, widen the filters. Four appearances are still required.

Ahmed may be playing two tournaments at once, but this meter only follows his TLPT results. The other tournament will need its own department of questionable decisions.
