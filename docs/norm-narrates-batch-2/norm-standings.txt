Welcome to the Standings. This is the league ledger, where a convincing story meets an inconvenient column.

Choose a button to rank the table by take-home, profit, cashes, bubbles, hits, or entries. Tap a player’s name for their profile. On your phone, swipe the table sideways for the rest of the evidence.

Take-home is what came back. Profit subtracts what went in, including bounty costs. Those are different numbers, despite what your postgame explanation may suggest.

Buy-ins count tournament appearances. Entries also include rebuys and reloads. Chris O and ProvidenceMike, just remember: a reload is another investment, not a fresh start for the accountant.

Use the event filters to compare formats or bounty events. They change the results shown, not career ratings or awards.
