Welcome to the Streak Tracker, where we distinguish a heater from a fond memory with excellent publicity.

A cash streak means consecutive matching appearances with a tournament cash. A drought means consecutive appearances without one. Collecting a bounty alone does not count as cashing.

Players need two matching appearances, and runs start at two. Missing a game neither breaks nor extends a streak. Events outside your chosen filters are skipped too.

Compare the active runs with the longest historical ones, then use Player Streak Lookup for dates and details.

“Active” means the run reaches that player’s most recent matching appearance. It does not mean they’re sitting at the table tonight.
