Welcome to Knockout Central. The league’s elimination records, arranged with just enough dignity to survive a group-chat screenshot.

Every recorded elimination is a hit, bounty or not. Bust somebody who later rebuys, and the hit still counts. A reload while they’re still alive is not an elimination.

Start with the leaders, then explore the Vendetta Ledger, nemeses, and Body Count. These show who eliminated whom, not who won every pot or delivered the loudest victory speech.

Use the filters to narrow the events. Switch to Bounty Hunters for the qualifying bounty subset and its money trail.
