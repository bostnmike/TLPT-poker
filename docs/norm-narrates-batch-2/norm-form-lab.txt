Welcome to the Form Lab. BostnMike has given a weekend poker league a research department. Apparently, the group chat wasn’t producing enough unsupported conclusions.

Start with a player, choose a preset, and pick the last six, last ten, or all matching appearances.

Each dot is one event. Select it to inspect that night’s results. The default chart compares event volatility with form score. Upper-left means stronger form with less modeled chaos. Change the axes, and you change what a good-looking corner means.

Open the chart options to customize the axes, labels, trend line, and player average.

The event filters narrow the sample before the window is applied. These are event-level measures, not somebody’s career Power rating.

Use the chart to ask better questions. A line through six dots is not a prophecy. Even if you drew it yourself.
