# Digital COA review — October 5, 2026

The owner confirmed that a puck’s authentication hologram identifies its digital certificate of authenticity. A separate paper certificate is not required. This convention applies to Fanatics, Bruins Authentics and other hologram-authenticated pucks; it must not be casually replaced with wording that questions provenance because paper is absent.

Reviewed all 170 listings, all 169 available puck-back photographs, 62 additional targeted views and the previously confirmed edge holograms for artifacts 32 and 77. Updated 80 records to “Digital COA / Hologram,” identifying the actual back/edge evidence. In total, revised 93 records and 63 gallery captions. No image files or gallery associations changed.

Artifact 70’s Fanatics hologram AA0016994 identifies its digital COA. GUHKPK1529 is the accompanying packaging identifier for the October 14, 2017 Boston–Arizona game. Packaging labels remain useful accompanying evidence and should not be confused with the puck’s hologram serial.

Removed generic authentication-limit language, including statements about undated paper certificates, shared same-game certificates and missing paper serials. Where authentication detail is awaiting database entry, describe that editorial follow-up without questioning the puck’s authenticity. Retained actual date, goal and serial discrepancies, all scoring facts, owner-approved catalog identifications and the distinction between game-used and scoring pucks.
