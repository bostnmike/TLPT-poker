How streaks are counted
Cash Streak Consecutive matching appearances that ended in a tournament cash.
Drought Consecutive matching appearances without a tournament cash.
Qualification Minimum 2 matching appearances. Only runs of 2+ count. Excluded and skipped events neither break nor extend a run.
